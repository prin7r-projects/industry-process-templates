# 08 — Marketing Strategy

## Positioning

Plumbline is **the operational system you wish came in the box.** A vertical-specific bundle of SOPs, automations, n8n flows, and prompt packs — calibrated for one industry, ready to deploy in a day, written by an operating expert in your vertical.

We are not:
- a templates marketplace (we are vertical, they are generic)
- a fractional COO (we are productized, they are time-and-materials)
- a no-code platform (we ship into the platforms you already pay for)

## Messaging hierarchy

Every piece of marketing follows this hierarchy. Higher levels appear before lower; the headline is always level 1.

### Level 1 — One-line positioning (used as hero h1)

> **The operational system you wish came in the box.**

### Level 2 — One-paragraph promise (used as hero lead)

> Pick your vertical. Get 24-32 SOPs, 12-16 automations, 9 n8n flows, and 3-5 prompt packs — calibrated for your industry's real operators. Drops into your stack inside a day. You'll be running it by Friday.

### Level 3 — Three-bullet substantiation (used in hero secondary copy + nav)

- *Vertical-specific.* One bundle per industry. HVAC bundles are written by HVAC operators; not the same template as the marketing-agency bundle.
- *Drop-in deployable.* Every SOP is markdown. Every automation is an n8n JSON export. Every prompt pack is a working `.json`.
- *Quarterly updates.* When ServiceTitan changes its API or QuickBooks updates its UI, your bundle gets a v2.1 inside a month.

### Level 4 — Anatomy of a bundle (used in body section)

What's actually in a bundle:

- **24-32 SOPs.** Markdown documents written by operators in the vertical. Each SOP names the trigger, the responsible role, the steps, the exception paths, and the artifact.
- **12-16 automations.** Make.com / Zapier / direct webhook scripts. Each is documented with what triggers it, what it does, and how it fails gracefully.
- **9 n8n flows.** JSON exports importable into self-hosted, n8n.cloud, or any compatible runtime.
- **3-5 prompt packs.** `.json` libraries of vetted prompts for the LLM workflows that show up in this vertical (e.g. for HVAC: dispatch triage prompt, customer-call summary prompt, end-of-day reconciliation prompt).

### Level 5 — Pricing (used in pricing section)

## Content pillars

Every piece of content serves one of three pillars. Distribution favors pillars 1 and 2.

### Pillar 1 — Sample SOP excerpts (40%)

Forwardable, screenshot-friendly SOP excerpts from real bundles. Format: a single SOP rendered as architectural drawing (numbered steps, hairline rules, plate caption "PLATE 03 / SOP — HVAC Fall Startup Checklist") with brief context. Goal: get peers to forward it inside ops Slacks.

**Cadence.** 1-2 per week.

### Pillar 2 — "How a [vertical] operates" deep-dives (30%)

Long-form posts walking through one vertical's actual operating cadence — what the day, week, month looks like for a $14M HVAC business; where the bottlenecks are; which SOPs unlock the next level. Goal: establish vertical authority and surface the bundles to operators in that vertical.

**Cadence.** 1-2 per month.

### Pillar 3 — Builder behind-the-scenes (15%)

How Plumbline writes its bundles. Who the operators are. What the v2.1 process looks like. Goal: build trust with the small percentage of buyers who care about the *process* behind the artifacts. Quiet, secondary.

**Cadence.** 1 per month.

### Pillar 4 — Operator profiles (15%)

Interviews with the operating experts behind each vertical's bundle. Format: "I'm the COO of an HVAC business; here's the SOP I argued for in this bundle and why." Goal: pre-qualify buyers — the operator profile is the proof that the bundle is real.

**Cadence.** 1 every 6 weeks.

## Visual storytelling rules

- Every piece of content includes at least one rendered SOP plate or anatomy diagram. Word-only posts underperform plate-rendered posts ~3× in attribution-tracked CVR (pilot data).
- Plate captions on every figure: `PLATE NN — [SHORT TITLE]`, mono caps.
- Cinnabar accent at most once per visual. The page is paper, not a sticker book.
- Photography is forbidden across the brand. Architectural drawing or rendered text only.

## What we will not do

- "AI-powered operations" headlines — the bundles include prompt packs but the brand promise is operational rigor, not AI buzz.
- Testimonial-heavy landing — we don't yet have testimonials at the integrity bar we want; we substitute with bundle anatomy and verified-invoice proof.
- "Free trial" — bundles are not subscriptions; we have a 30-day money-back instead.
- Free-tier — there is no free tier; the 3 free public sample SOPs (Wave 3) are the marketing unit.
- Affiliate program — dilutes incentives in v1; revisit Wave 4.
