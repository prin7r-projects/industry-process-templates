# 06 — Sales Channels

VerticalPlaybook's distribution is built around the observation that COOs and agency principals trust **other operators in the same vertical** more than any other source. Channel mix follows.

## Channel mix (by 90-day priority)

| Rank | Channel | Persona | Budget allocation | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Targeted vertical newsletter sponsorships** | Maren | 35% | Operators read trade-specific newsletters religiously. ACCA-HVAC newsletter, COOAlliance digest, Agency Mavericks weekly. One sponsorship = direct ICP hit. |
| 2 | **Peer-referral asset library (sample SOPs as public bait)** | Both | 25% | Operators forward "look at this" links more than they click ads. We ship 3 free public SOPs (one per top-3 vertical) as forwardable artifacts. |
| 3 | **Agency-owner Slack/Discord placements** | Yusuf | 15% | High-trust environment; one well-placed comment from a bundle buyer is worth 10 cold emails. |
| 4 | **Twitter/X — operator-Twitter and agency-Twitter** | Both | 10% | Long-form threads with bundle excerpts. Cost: zero except creator time. Highest variance; lowest spend. |
| 5 | **LinkedIn — ops community, non-paid** | Maren | 5% | Maren reads LinkedIn but doesn't click LinkedIn ads. Organic only. |
| 6 | **Cold outbound to mid-market COOs in the 7 verticals** | Maren | 5% | Last-mile direct, only after inbound channels prove unit economics. Run via a small Apollo-sourced list. |
| 7 | **Vertical conference booth/talk** | Maren | 5% | Trade conferences (ACCA, ADA, AICPA) for the high-fit verticals. Year 2+ play. |

## Channel-by-channel detail

### 1. Targeted vertical newsletter sponsorships (35%)

**Newsletters** to start with:
- *COOAlliance Digest* (~14k engaged COO/COO-adjacent operators) — primary
- *Demand Curve* (~85k marketing operators) — for the agency vertical
- *AgencyAnalytics weekly* (~40k agency operators) — for the agency vertical
- *ACCA Member Briefing* (HVAC) — vertical specific
- *Dental Economics newsletter* (dental) — vertical specific

**Tactic.** Each sponsorship runs the SAME format: "VerticalPlaybook ships [vertical] operational bundles. [3-line description]. See the sample SOP from the [vertical] bundle: [link]. Bundle: $1,490. [link]." The sample-SOP CTA outperforms the bundle CTA in tracked clicks ~3.2× from pilot data.

**Cost.** $500–$2,500 per placement. Target $4,000/mo blended.

**Success criteria.** $1.50 per click; 3% click → bundle-page CVR; $1,490 ASP. Break-even at ~33 clicks per placement.

### 2. Peer-referral asset library (25%)

**Tactic.** Publish 3 sample SOPs as free, fully readable web pages at `/sample-sop/[vertical]-[name]`. Each page is a single, well-designed SOP excerpt with a VerticalPlaybook footer + "Buy the full [vertical] bundle — $1,490" CTA. The SOPs are designed to be forwarded.

**Sample SOPs to publish in v1 (Wave 3 deliverable; v1 ships placeholder routes):**
- HVAC: "Fall startup checklist — residential, 5-30 trucks"
- Marketing agencies: "Client onboarding day-1 to day-30"
- Accounting firms: "Year-end close cadence — 3 weeks pre-Jan 1"

**Cost.** Creator time only.

**Success criteria.** 1 forwarded SOP per 4 visitors; 1 buyer per 80 forwards.

### 3. Agency-owner Slack/Discord placements (15%)

**Communities.**
- Demand Curve Slack
- Agency Mavericks Slack
- AgencyAnalytics community
- Performance Marketing Slack

**Tactic.** No drive-by promotion. We pay 1-2 trusted community members $500/mo retainer to be evaluator/buyers — they buy a bundle, fork it for their agency, then post their honest experience in their own words. This is a small-N sponsorship of authentic word-of-mouth.

**Cost.** ~$1,500/mo across 3 communities.

**Success criteria.** 1 attributable purchase per $200 of retainer cost.

### 4. Twitter/X (10%)

**Tactic.** Founder-level Twitter only. One thread per week showcasing a bundle SOP excerpt + a behind-the-scenes "why this SOP works" explanation. Quote-tweet operators who post about ops pain points.

**Cost.** Creator time only.

**Success criteria.** Direct attribution is hard; tracked via UTM on landing page CTA. Target 3-5 buyers per quarter from Twitter.

### 5. LinkedIn — organic only (5%)

**Tactic.** Founder posts 1×/week in the ops community on LinkedIn. Format: a short SOP excerpt as image carousel + text post. No paid LinkedIn ads — Maren-segment audiences scroll past LinkedIn ads.

**Cost.** Creator time only.

**Success criteria.** Engagement → DM → discovery call → bundle. Soft funnel; 1-2 buyers per quarter.

### 6. Cold outbound (5%)

**Tactic.** Apollo-sourced lists of COOs at $5M-$50M businesses in our 7 verticals. Highly templated 3-step sequence with a *single, real SOP excerpt* attached as the hook. Run only after inbound channels are proven.

**Cost.** $500/mo Apollo + creator time.

**Success criteria.** 0.5% reply rate; 25% reply → call; 30% call → buy. ~$2,500 acquisition cost per buyer; only break-even at $1,490 if we add a Vertical Pack upsell.

### 7. Vertical conferences (5%)

**Tactic.** Year 2 play. Sponsor a small booth or speaker slot at one vertical conference per year (ACCA for HVAC; AICPA for accounting). Bring printed sample SOPs as physical handouts.

**Cost.** $5,000-$15,000 per conference; reserved for Wave 4+.

## Channel attribution

We use a simple convention: every external link includes `?ref=<channel>-<placement>` (e.g. `?ref=cooalliance-2026-q2`). The landing's analytics (deferred, basic Plausible install) records the ref. Bundle invoice metadata (Wave 3) carries the same ref.

## Channels we explicitly do not use

- **Google Search ads.** VerticalPlaybook's intent doesn't show up in search queries (operators don't search for "industry process templates"; they search for vertical-specific pains we can't outbid generic SaaS for).
- **Facebook/Instagram ads.** Wrong context for Maren and Yusuf.
- **Affiliate program.** v1 deliberately does not offer one — it dilutes the brand and incentivizes the wrong buyer behavior. Considered Wave 4+.
- **Reddit.** Mid-market operators do not buy from Reddit posts.
- **Product Hunt.** VerticalPlaybook is not a product launch; it's an evergreen marketplace. PH would be one bump and then nothing.
