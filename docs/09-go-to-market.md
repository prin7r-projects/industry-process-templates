# 09 — Go-to-market (90-day plan)

## Stance

The 90-day plan is built around proving VerticalPlaybook's three economic premises:

1. **A vertical-specific bundle outperforms a generic templates pack on conversion at price.** Goal: $1,490 ASP at 2-3% CVR from qualified-traffic landing pages.
2. **Peer-to-peer referral is the dominant channel.** Goal: >60% of buyers in days 60-90 come from non-paid channels.
3. **Quarterly updates retain buyers.** Goal: <20% churn-equivalent on annual update renewals.

The plan is sequenced so that paid channels prove unit economics first, then peer-referral and content compound.

## Targets

- 90-day revenue: ~$57,000
- 90-day buyers: ~38 (mix: ~22 single-bundle, ~13 vertical-pack, ~3 enterprise)
- 90-day cost: ~$15,000 (sponsorships + community retainers + tools)
- 90-day CAC blended: <$400
- 90-day non-paid % of buyers (days 60-90): >60%

## Risk register

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Low CVR on landing — bundle promise unclear | Iterate bundle-anatomy section; add bundle ".zip preview" download (Wave 3); A/B sample-SOP excerpts on hero |
| One vertical dominates and others starve | Allocate sponsorship spend evenly across top 3 verticals for first 8 weeks regardless of CVR |
| Peer-reviewer cohort doesn't deliver public reviews | Renew the 5-peer cohort by Wk-4 and recruit a second cohort by Wk-6 |
| NOWPayments availability blip | Manual Plisio fallback; documented in 02-architecture |
| Bundle quality issue surfaces post-purchase | 30-day refund policy; refund processed in stablecoin within 5 business days |
| Vertical-fit mismatch (buyer in wrong vertical) | Tighten "fits if" definitions; add a 2-question quiz to landing pre-CTA in Wave 3 |

## What we are not measuring

- Vanity metrics: page views, follower count
- Newsletter open rates outside our own Vertical Reports newsletter
- Brand awareness — we are too small for it to matter
- Net Promoter Score — fewer than 100 buyers makes this noise
